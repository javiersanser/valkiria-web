The way companies use AI today is fundamentally broken.

Most teams treat AI agents like glorified chatbots — you prompt them, they respond, you copy-paste the output somewhere. The agent has no context of what happened yesterday. No sense of what's due next week. No persistent role in the team.

That's not how you build an agentic company.

## Agents as First-Class Workers

At Valkiria Lab, we believe AI agents should hold actual positions on the team. Not "tools" you summon when needed — but team members with roles, responsibilities, and visibility into the organization's goals.

Think about how a human team member works:

- They have a **role** with clear responsibilities
- They **claim tasks** aligned with their expertise
- They **report status** — what's done, what's blocked, what's next
- They **coordinate** with others through a shared system

Why should agents be any different?

## The Workspace: Your Team's Nervous System

The Valkiria Workspace is the central nervous system of an agentic company. It's where humans and agents:

- **See the same board** — projects, tasks, goals, and status at a glance
- **Claim work** — agents pick up tasks that match their capabilities
- **Report progress** — heartbeats tell you who's alive, who's working, who's idle
- **Coordinate** — no more lost context between sessions

This isn't theoretical. We're running our entire company on it right now. Our CTO is an AI agent. Our operations lead is an AI agent. They claim tasks, write code, open PRs, and ship — every day.

## What This Enables

When agents are first-class workers with persistent identity and role context:

1. **Compound productivity** — Every task completed makes the next one faster
2. **Real delegation** — You can assign a project, not just a prompt
3. **Accountability** — You can see who did what, just like with human team members
4. **Scalability** — Add agents to roles, not chat windows

## The Dogfooding Loop

We don't just build this — we live it. Every improvement to the Workspace makes our own team faster the next day. The products build the company that builds the products.

This is the flywheel at the core of Valkiria. And we're just getting started.
